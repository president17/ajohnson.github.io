About Me

I'm not a web developer, though that may be obvious - depends who's looking at this page, I suppose. So, who am I?

I earned my bachelor's in Mechanical Engineering at WashU, and afterwards my Masters in Materials Science and Engineering. I minored in Robotics, but most of my experience really comes from the Robotics Club and the robotics I did before college too. When I wasn't sticking myself in the unfinished basement space we called "the shop," I enjoyed running and rock clibming as a student. Outside of my university education, I have interned at a silicon wafer manufacturing plant, lived in Ireland for a semester, and lived off a kayak in Canada for a month. The forested dunes of western Michigan are as much my home as anywhere I have legally resided.
