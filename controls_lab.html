Control Systems Design Lab

In my design course, ESE4480: Control Systems Design Laboratory, my partner and I were tasked with controlling and balancing a two-link inverted pendulum system. To accomplish this, we would program the second link to swing up from rest and then “catch” the swinging link at the peak of rotation. The vertical position can be stable, even as the base rotates beneath. To accomplish this, we followed a structure: model, measure parameters, and design a controller. The motion was modeled with kinematic and dynamic equations, and the parameters recorded by encoders on the joints of each link. The controller had three modes: swinging the pendulum up, balancing it, and waiting for it to fall. This allowed it to recover and try to balance again without any help. We were able to achieve stable balanced and rotational control of the base, an extra step to ensure our design was sound. To simulate the pendulum and control it, we used MATLAB and its accompanying Simulink. The video shown explains the project in more detail and the full working result.
